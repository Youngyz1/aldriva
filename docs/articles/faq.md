# Articles & Publishing FAQ

---

## General Questions

### What is the difference between an Article and a Campaign Update?
An **Article** is a standalone editorial publication indexed across the global platform, suitable for in-depth storytelling, thought leadership, event recaps, and investigative impact reports. A **Campaign Update** is a chronological status post attached directly to a single fundraiser page.

### Who can publish articles on Aldriva?
Any authenticated user with an active Aldriva account in good standing can create and submit articles. Authors managing verified Organizations can choose to publish stories under their organization's official brand.

### Does publishing an article cost money?
No. Creating, publishing, embedding entity cards, and using the AI writing assistant on Aldriva is completely free for registered authors and organizers.

## AI Assistant & Writing Tools

### Will the AI automatically publish or overwrite my content?
Never. The AI Writing Assistant is strictly an author aid. All generated drafts, improvements, and metadata suggestions appear in interactive preview dialogs. Authors must explicitly review and accept suggestions.

### Is there a limit on AI Assistant usage?
To ensure service reliability, authors can make up to 30 AI requests per minute. Standard authors rarely exceed this limit during normal writing sessions.

### Can I use AI to write an entire article from scratch?
Yes. You can select **AI-Assisted Draft** when creating a new article, supply key bullet points, audience context, and desired tone, and the assistant will generate a structured initial draft.

## Editorial Review & Moderation

### Why is my article status listed as "Pending Review"?
To protect the community from spam, fraudulent fundraising links, and harmful content, all submitted articles pass through an administrative review queue before going live.

### How long does the review process take?
Most articles are reviewed and approved within a few hours during standard operating periods.

### Can I edit an article after it has been published?
Yes. You can edit your article at any time from **Dashboard → My Articles → Edit**. Minor typographical edits update instantly; significant changes to core metadata may trigger a secondary review.

## Media, Audio & Embedding

### What are the recommended dimensions for cover images?
We recommend images with a **16:9 aspect ratio** and minimum dimensions of **1200 x 675 pixels** (JPEG, PNG, or WebP).

### How does the Audio Narration feature work?
Aldriva automatically synthesizes spoken audio versions for published articles, enabling readers to listen on mobile devices or accessibility screen readers. Audio progress synchronizes with paragraph highlights.

### How do I embed a fundraiser or event card into my article?
Click the **Entity Card** button in the rich-text editor toolbar, search for your target fundraiser or event by name, and click **Embed**.
