# Payouts & Withdrawals

This guide outlines how organizers and beneficiaries configure payout destinations, request withdrawals, and receive funds raised on Aldriva.

---

## ⚙️ Configuring Payout Destinations

To manage payment and payout settings:
1. Navigate to **Settings -> Payments** (`/dashboard/settings/payments`).
2. Select your default payout currency (e.g., USD).
3. Set your preferred payout schedule (e.g., weekly or manual requests).
4. Add your payout destination:
   - **Bank Transfer (Direct Deposit / ACH)**: Bank routing number and account number.
   - **Debit Card**: Eligible Visa/Mastercard for instant transfers.
   - **Crypto Wallet**: Supported wallet address for digital currency payouts.

---

## 💸 Requesting a Payout

To request a withdrawal:
1. Open the **Payments** page in your dashboard.
2. Check your **Available Balance**.
3. Click **Request Payout**.
4. Enter the amount you wish to withdraw and select your payout destination.
5. Click **Submit Request**.

---

## 🚦 Payout Statuses & Lifecycle

| Payout Status | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Requested / Pending** | Payout request submitted and queued for processing. |
| **Processing** | Bank transfer or payment gateway transfer in transit. |
| **Completed** | Funds successfully deposited into your destination account. |
| **Failed** | Payout could not be completed (e.g., invalid routing number); funds return to available balance. |
| **Cancelled** | Payout request was cancelled by the organizer prior to processing. |

---

## 🛡️ Security & Identity Verification

- Payouts require the organizer to complete identity verification (`/dashboard/settings/verification`).
- Funds from recent card donations may undergo standard clearing windows before becoming available for withdrawal to prevent chargebacks.
