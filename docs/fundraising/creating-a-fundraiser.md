# Creating a Fundraiser

This guide walks organizers through setting up, configuring, and publishing a fundraising campaign on Aldriva.

---

## 🚀 Quick Start: 4-Step Creation Wizard

To start a campaign, visit `/create-fundraiser` or click **Create Fundraiser** in your dashboard:

1. **Step 1: Fundraiser Details**: Choose between a Personal or Organization campaign, enter your campaign title, select a category, and write a short summary.
2. **Step 2: Goal & Story**: Set your target fundraising goal in USD and write your detailed story using the rich-text editor.
3. **Step 3: Media & Settings**: Upload up to 8 cropped photos (4:5 aspect ratio) or a campaign video, and configure beneficiary details.
4. **Step 4: Review & Publish**: Preview your campaign, save as a local draft if needed, and submit to launch.

---

## 📋 Campaign Types: Personal vs. Organization

Aldriva supports two distinct campaign structures:

### 1. Personal Fundraisers
- **Who Can Create**: Any authenticated Aldriva user.
- **Organizer**: Set to your personal user account.
- **Beneficiary**: Automatically defaults to yourself.
- **Approval**: No organization verification required.

### 2. Organization Fundraisers
- **Who Can Create**: Users who own or manage a registered organization profile.
- **Organizer**: Linked to your approved organization profile.
- **Requirement**: The organization must be approved for fundraising (`fundraising_approved: true`).
- **Beneficiary**: Can be designated to the organization, an individual, a family, or another entity.

## 📝 Crafting Your Story & Adding Media

### 1. Goal Setting
- Set a realistic fundraising target in USD.
- If you are migrating a campaign from another platform, you can record an initial seed raised amount.

### 2. Rich Text Story
- Use the built-in rich text editor to format paragraphs, bold text, bullet points, and headings.
- Explain:
  1. Who or what this fundraiser supports.
  2. Why the funds are urgently needed.
  3. Exactly how the donations will be allocated.

### 3. Media Guidelines
- **Photos**: Upload up to **8 photos**. Images are automatically formatted to the standard **4:5 aspect ratio** to ensure sharp display on mobile devices.
- **Cover Photo**: The first uploaded photo automatically serves as the primary banner and social sharing thumbnail.
- **Video**: Optionally upload a video file (.mp4, .mov, .webm) hosted directly on the platform.

---

## 💾 Saving Drafts & Submitting

- **Save Draft**: Click **Save Draft** at any step to save your in-progress campaign details locally in your browser.
- **Publishing**: When submitted, your campaign slug is generated (e.g., `/fundraisers/help-johnson-family`) and your campaign becomes active.
