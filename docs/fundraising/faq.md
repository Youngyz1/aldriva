# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find answers to common questions about creating, managing, and donating to fundraisers on Aldriva.

---

## 🎪 For Campaign Organizers

### Q: Who can create a fundraiser on Aldriva?
**A**: Any registered user can create a **Personal Fundraiser** immediately. To launch an **Organization Fundraiser**, you must link an approved organization profile.

### Q: How much does it cost to create a fundraiser?
**A**: Creating and hosting a campaign on Aldriva is free. Standard credit card processing fees apply to incoming donations, and donors have the option to leave an optional tip to support platform operations.

### Q: Can I edit my goal amount after launching?
**A**: Yes. You can adjust your target fundraising goal at any time in the campaign edit page (`/fundraisers/edit/[id]`).

### Q: Can I raise money for someone else?
**A**: Yes. When creating an organization campaign, select **Another person** or **Family** as the beneficiary. You can also send them a secure link to claim their beneficiary profile.

### Q: How do I withdraw funds raised?
**A**: Go to **Settings -> Payments** (`/dashboard/settings/payments`), select your payout destination (Bank Transfer, Card, or Crypto Wallet), and click **Request Payout**.

---

## 💖 For Donors

### Q: Can I donate anonymously?
**A**: Yes. On the donation checkout screen, check the box **"Don't display my name publicly on the fundraiser"**. Your name will appear as "Anonymous" on the public donor list.

### Q: Do I get a tax receipt for my donation?
**A**: Yes. Immediately upon donation, you can download an official PDF receipt. Donations made to verified 501(c)(3) registered charities include the organization's Tax ID and registration number.

### Q: Is tipping mandatory?
**A**: No. The platform tip slider can be set to any percentage from 0% to 30%, or you can enter a custom amount. Setting the tip to 0% is fully supported.

---

## 👥 For Beneficiaries

### Q: I received a link to claim a beneficiary profile. What does this do?
**A**: Claiming your profile links the campaign to your Aldriva account, giving you official verification and direct access to your beneficiary portal at `/dashboard/beneficiary`.
