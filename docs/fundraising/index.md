# Aldriva Fundraising Documentation

Welcome to the comprehensive user documentation for **Aldriva Fundraising** — an intuitive, secure platform for personal campaigns, nonprofit initiatives, and community fundraising.

---

## 🧭 Documentation Map by Topic

### 🚀 Creating & Launching Campaigns
- **[Creating a Fundraiser](creating-a-fundraiser.md)**: Set up personal or organization campaigns, choose categories, write stories, add photos/videos, and publish.
- **[Beneficiary Setup & Claims](beneficiaries.md)**: Designate who the fundraiser benefits (yourself, individuals, families, charities, or groups) and send profile claim invitations.
- **[Verification & Trust](verification-and-trust.md)**: Complete organizer verification, fundraising approval requirements, and understand moderation states.

### 💖 Donating & Receipts
- **[Donating to a Campaign](donating.md)**: The donor journey: custom amounts, optional platform tips, anonymous giving, words of support, and payment options.
- **[Donation Receipts & Tax Information](receipts-and-tax.md)**: Access official PDF receipts, 501(c)(3) tax deduction details, and receipt lookup tools.

### 📊 Managing Campaigns & Donors
- **[Managing Your Fundraisers](managing-fundraisers.md)**: Monitor campaign stats, edit details, post updates to donors, and delete campaigns.
- **[Donations Dashboard](donations-dashboard.md)**: Track incoming transactions, view donor details, filter by campaign/date, and export CSV logs.
- **[Payouts & Withdrawals](payouts-and-withdrawals.md)**: Configure payout destinations (bank transfer, card, crypto wallet), schedule payouts, and withdraw funds.

### 📢 Promotion & Support
- **[Sharing & Discovery](sharing-and-discovery.md)**: Maximize visibility through dynamic social preview cards, direct links, and public browse categories.
- **[Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)](faq.md)**: Quick answers to common questions for organizers, donors, and beneficiaries.
- **[Troubleshooting Guide](troubleshooting.md)**: Diagnostic steps for creation errors, payment declines, verification holds, and receipt issues.
