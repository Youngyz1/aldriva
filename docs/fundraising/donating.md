# Donating to a Campaign

This guide explains how donors discover, contribute to, and support campaigns on Aldriva.

---

## 💖 The Donation Workflow

```
1. Open Campaign Page (/fundraisers/[slug])
               │
               ▼
2. Click "Donate Now" (/fundraisers/[slug]/donate)
               │
               ▼
3. Enter Donation Amount ($ USD)
               │
               ▼
4. Choose Platform Tip Percentage (0% to 30%)
               │
               ▼
5. Enter Donor Details (Name, Email, Anonymous toggle)
               │
               ▼
6. Add optional Words of Support / Message
               │
               ▼
7. Secure Checkout via Stripe (Credit/Debit Card) or Crypto
               │
               ▼
8. Instant Confirmation Screen & Downloadable PDF Receipt
```

---

## 💵 Donation Form Options

When completing a donation at `/fundraisers/[slug]/donate`, donors configure the following:

### 1. Donation Amount
- Custom amount typed by the donor in USD (minimum $1.00).

### 2. Platform Tip Slider
- Donors can choose an optional tip percentage between **0% and 30%** (or type a custom exact percentage) to help cover Aldriva platform operations and payment processing.
- Setting the tip to 0% is fully supported.

### 3. Anonymous Giving
- **"Don't display my name publicly on the fundraiser"**: Checking this box marks your donation as **Anonymous** on the public donor list. Your name is visible only to the campaign organizer for accounting purposes.

### 4. Words of Support & Comments
- Donors can leave an encouraging message. Approved messages are publicly displayed in the **Words of Support** section on the fundraiser page.

---

## 💳 Payment Methods Supported

- **Credit & Debit Cards**: Major cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover) processed securely via Stripe.
- **Crypto Payments**: Supported cryptocurrency contributions where enabled.

---

## 📄 Post-Donation Confirmation & Receipts

Immediately after payment:
1. The donor is presented with a confirmation receipt on screen.
2. A formal **Donation Receipt** is generated with a unique reference ID.
3. Donors can instantly download an official PDF receipt for tax or reimbursement records.
4. If the donor gave to a verified 501(c)(3) nonprofit, the receipt includes the nonprofit's tax ID and registration number.
