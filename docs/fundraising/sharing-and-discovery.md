# Sharing & Discovery

Maximizing campaign visibility is critical to reaching your fundraising goals. Aldriva provides automated sharing tools and public discovery channels.

---

## 🔗 Public Campaign URLs

Every published fundraiser receives a clean, readable public URL:

```
https://aldriva.com/fundraisers/[campaign-slug]
```

---

## 🖼️ Dynamic OpenGraph Social Preview Cards

When you share your campaign URL on social media (Facebook, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, iMessage):
- Aldriva automatically renders a dynamic OpenGraph image showing your cover photo, campaign title, current amount raised, target goal, and live progress percentage ring.
- As new donations arrive, social preview cards reflect updated totals.

---

## 📢 Built-in Social Share Tools

On your public campaign page, click **Share Fundraiser** to access 1-click sharing options:
- **Copy Link**: Copies the clean direct URL to your clipboard.
- **WhatsApp**: Opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled campaign message.
- **X (Twitter)**: Drafts a tweet with campaign hashtags.
- **Facebook**: Opens the Facebook share dialog.
- **LinkedIn**: Shares directly to your professional network.
- **Embed Code**: Provides an HTML embed snippet to display a mini campaign card on external websites or blogs.

---

## 🔍 Public Browse & Search Discovery

Published campaigns appear on Aldriva's public discovery portal (`/fundraisers`):
- **Category Browsing**: Visitors can browse campaigns by Medical, Emergency, Charity, Community, Education, etc.
- **Keyword Search**: Search by campaign title, keywords, or organizer name.
- **Featured Campaigns**: High-impact campaigns featured by platform curators.
