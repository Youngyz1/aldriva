# Event Team

Event staff are people you invite to help run an event.

## Staff roles
The current public role names are:
- **Event Manager**
- **Ticket Scanner**

## What each role can do
- **Event Manager**: manage attendance, view check-ins, manage the team, and scan tickets
- **Ticket Scanner**: scan tickets at the door
- **Organizer**: full event control, including event details and staff management

## How staff access works
1. The organizer sends an invitation to a staff email address
2. The staff member signs in to Aldriva
3. The staff member accepts the invitation
4. Aldriva connects that account to the event

The invitation must be accepted with the same email address it was sent to.

## How organizers use staff
Organizers use staff to:
- Share door duties
- Separate scanning from broader event management
- Give trusted helpers access without sharing organizer access

## Managing staff
From the event team page, organizers can:
- Invite new staff
- Resend invitations
- Cancel pending invitations
- Remove active staff

## What happens when access is removed
If a staff member is removed from an event, they immediately lose access to that event's staff tools.
