# QR Tickets

## What is a QR ticket?
A QR ticket is the scannable version of your ticket pass. Event staff scan it at the door to verify entry.

## Why does each ticket have its own QR code?
Each ticket is checked in one person at a time. Giving every ticket its own QR code makes it possible to:
- Check in people independently
- Track attendance accurately
- Prevent the same ticket from being used twice

## If I buy multiple tickets, how many QR codes do I receive?
You receive one QR code per ticket.

Examples:
- 1 ticket = 1 QR code
- 5 tickets = 5 QR codes

## Can I use a ticket for another event?
No. A ticket is tied to the event it was purchased for.

A ticket for Event A cannot be used to enter Event B.

## Can the same QR code be used again?
Not after check-in. Once a ticket has been checked in, it is treated as used and cannot be scanned again for another entry.

Re-entry tracking is not currently available.

## Are QR codes dynamic?
No. The QR code for a ticket is fixed for that ticket.

## Current limits
Not currently supported:
- Ticket transfer
- Dynamic QR codes
- Re-entry tracking
