# Organization Profiles & Public Hubs

Every organization on Aldriva has a public profile hub located at `/org/[slug]`. It serves as the single home for your community presence, showcasing your upcoming events, active fundraisers, community reviews, and social channels.

---

## 🌐 Public URL & Navigation

- **Canonical URL**: `https://aldriva.com/org/[your-slug]`
- **Automatic Redirects**: If visitors access legacy URLs (such as `/organizations/[slug]`) or use your organization's internal UUID, Aldriva automatically redirects them to your canonical slug.

---

## 🏛️ Public Hub Layout & Features

The public hub consists of a header overview and four modular sections:

### 1. Header & Trust Banner
- **Logo & Name**: Displays your square organization avatar and official name.
- **Organization Type Badge**: Indicates your entity classification (e.g., Nonprofit, Business, Church).
- **Verified Badge**: A prominent check badge displayed if your entity has completed [Organization Verification](organization-verification.md).
- **Star Rating Rollup**: Displays your average star rating and review count (e.g., `★ 4.9 (18 reviews)`).
- **Follow Button**: Community members can follow your organization to receive real-time updates when you publish new events or campaigns.
- **Share Button**: Copy a direct link to your public hub.
- **Contact Button**: Direct email link to your public contact address.

### 2. Overview Tab
The default landing view designed to give visitors a quick snapshot:
- **Next Upcoming Event**: A featured card highlighting your next scheduled event with date, venue, city, and ticket link.
- **Active Campaigns**: Cards displaying your top active fundraisers with real-time progress bars, total raised, and goal targets.
- **Key Metrics**: Summary totals for total events hosted, active campaigns, followers, and total funds raised.

### 3. Events Tab
- Displays all public events hosted by your organization.
- **Upcoming Events**: Active events with ticket purchasing links and venue details.
- **Past Events**: Historical record of completed events.

### 4. Campaigns Tab
- Full catalog of all active fundraising campaigns linked to your organization, featuring interactive progress bars, goals, and direct donation buttons.

### 5. About & Connect (Sidebar / Rail)
- **About Mission**: Complete organization biography and mission statement.
- **Website & Email**: Verified external website and contact email.
- **Social Media Links**: Direct icons for Facebook, X (Twitter), Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, and TikTok.

---

## 📱 Public Hub vs. Management Dashboard

| Feature | Public Hub (`/org/[slug]`) | Organization Dashboard (`/dashboard/org/[id]`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Audience** | Public visitors, attendees, donors | Organization owners and managers |
| **Access** | Open to everyone | Authenticated owners/managers only |
| **Content** | Published events, active campaigns, bio | Analytics, draft management, verification wizard |
| **Editing** | Read-only | Full editing and configuration controls |
