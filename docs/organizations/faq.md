# Profiles, Organizations, & Identity/Trust FAQ

Frequently asked questions about user profiles, organization hubs, verification, following, and community trust on Aldriva.

---

## 👤 User Profiles & Privacy

### How do I change my display name or avatar?
Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Profile** (`/dashboard/settings/profile`). You can upload a new photo, use the cropping tool, update your display name, and click **Save Profile Details**.

### Why is my donation showing on my public profile?
Public profiles display contributions made to published campaigns where you did not select the "Donate anonymously" checkbox. If you prefer your donations to remain private, select "Donate anonymously" during checkout or disable **Display Donation Badges** in **Settings → Privacy**.

### Can other people see my email or address?
No. Your email address, phone numbers, and physical addresses are strictly private and are never shown on your public profile.

## 🏢 Organizations & Public Hubs

### What is the difference between a personal account and an organization?
A personal account represents you as an individual member. An organization represents a collective entity (such as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, business, church, school, or creator brand) with its own public hub at `/org/[slug]`, verified badges, and multi-event hosting capabilities.

### Can I create multiple organizations?
Yes. A single Aldriva user account can create and manage multiple organizations from the dashboard.

### What are the "Coming Soon" sections in the organization dashboard?
The **Volunteers**, **Gallery**, **Services**, and **Analytics** modules under `/dashboard/org/[id]` are scheduled for future development. They currently display a "Coming Soon" placeholder.

## 🛡️ Verification & Trust Badges

### What is the difference between Identity Verification and Organization Verification?
- **Personal Identity Verification** confirms your personal identity using a government ID and is required for payout withdrawals.
- **Organization Verification** accredits your organization (nonprofit, business, church) using official entity registration documents to grant the public `🛡️ Verified` badge and fundraising approval.

### How long does verification take?
Submissions enter the administrative review queue immediately upon submission. You can check your real-time status in your dashboard.

### What if my verification submission is marked "Needs More Info"?
If a reviewer needs clearer scans or additional documentation, your submission will update to **Needs More Info**. Simply open the verification page, read the reviewer notes, upload the requested documents, and re-submit.

---

## 🤝 Following & Reviews

### Can I follow my own organization?
No. To ensure authentic social metrics, self-following is disabled.

### Can I edit or delete a review I submitted?
Currently, each user can submit one review per target. If you need to update or remove feedback, contact platform support.
