# Personal Identity Verification

Personal Identity Verification confirms the real-world identity of account owners on Aldriva. It safeguards the platform against fraudulent activities and is required before an individual can withdraw payout funds from personal fundraisers or manage financial distributions.

---

## 🎯 Why Complete Identity Verification?

- **Payout & Withdrawal Access**: Required to configure direct deposit bank transfers or crypto payouts for campaigns you manage.
- **Account Security**: Protects your account against unauthorized takeover or impersonation.
- **Platform Trust**: Establishes accountability for campaign organizers across the community.

---

## 📄 Supported Government Identification

Aldriva accepts valid, government-issued photo identification:

1. **National Identity Card**: Official government-issued citizen or residence identity card.
2. **Passport**: Valid international passport photo page.
3. **Driver's License**: Government-issued driver's license (front photo side).

---

## 📤 Step-by-Step Verification Process

### Step 1: Open the Verification Wizard
1. Log in and navigate to **Settings → Verification** (`/dashboard/settings/verification`).
2. Under **Identity Verification**, click **Verify Identity** (`/dashboard/settings/verification/identity`).

### Step 2: Select Document Type & Upload
1. Choose your document type: **National ID**, **Passport**, or **Driver's License**.
2. Prepare a clear, high-resolution photo or scan of your document:
   - Ensure all four corners of the ID are visible.
   - Text, name, date of birth, and expiration date must be legible.
   - Avoid glare, reflections, or heavy shadows.
   - Supported formats: **PDF, JPG, JPEG, PNG, WebP** (up to 10MB per file).
3. Upload the document using the secure upload tool.

### Step 3: Add Notes (Optional) & Submit
1. Provide any helpful notes for the verification team (e.g., name change documentation if applicable).
2. Click **Submit for Verification**.

---

## 🔄 Verification Statuses Explained

| Status | Meaning | What to Do |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Draft** | Your verification draft has been started but not submitted. | Complete document upload and click Submit. |
| **Submitted** | Your documents are queued for administrative review. | No action needed. Review is underway. |
| **Verified (Approved)** | Identity confirmed. Payouts and high-trust actions unlocked. | You are fully verified! |
| **Needs More Info** | The reviewer requested clearer or additional documents. | Open the wizard, review notes, upload new files, and re-submit. |
| **Rejected** | The submission did not meet verification criteria. | Read the feedback notes and start a new submission with valid ID. |

---

## 🔒 Document Privacy & Storage Security

- **Strict Confidentiality**: Your uploaded identity documents are stored in private, encrypted cloud storage.
- **Zero Public Exposure**: Documents are **never** accessible to public visitors, event attendees, or donors.
- **Restricted Access**: Only authorized platform compliance reviewers can inspect uploaded verification files.
