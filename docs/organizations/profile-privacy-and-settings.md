# Profile Privacy & Account Settings

Aldriva gives you granular control over what information is visible to the public, search engines, and other community members.

---

## ⚙️ Accessing Privacy Settings

Navigate to **Dashboard → Settings → Privacy** (`/dashboard/settings/privacy`) to configure your visibility preferences.

---

## 🛡️ Discoverability & Search Indexing

### Profile Status (Public vs. Private)
- **Public (Default)**: Your profile page at `/profile/[id]` is accessible to community members, displaying your public campaigns, organizations, and public impact.
- **Private**: Hides your public profile details from general member browsing.

### Search Engine Indexing
- **Allow Search Indexing (Toggle)**: Controls whether major search engines (Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo) are permitted to index your public profile and public organization pages.
- **Disabled**: Adds standard search crawler exclusion directives to your public pages.

---

## 👁️ Content & Activity Privacy Toggles

You can toggle specific content items on or off:

| Privacy Toggle | Description | Default |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Display Email** | Displays your contact email address on your public organization page. When disabled, your email is never shown publicly. | Disabled (Off) |
| **Show Organized Events** | Lists your active, public events on your organization profile. When disabled, events are hidden from your profile overview. | Enabled (On) |
| **Display Donation Badges** | Displays your name and contribution amount badge on public fundraiser donor feeds. When disabled, your contributions appear anonymously. | Enabled (On) |

---

## 🔐 Guaranteed Private Information

The following data is strictly confidential and is **never** broadcasted or exposed through public APIs or profile pages:
- **Personal Identification**: Government ID photos, passport scans, national ID numbers, and verification documents.
- **Physical Addresses**: Home, billing, shipping, and work address records stored in your profile.
- **Financial & Payment Data**: Credit card tokens, crypto wallet details, bank account credentials, and payout transaction histories.
- **Anonymous Donations**: Any contribution where you selected the "Donate anonymously" checkbox during checkout.
- **Draft & In-Review Content**: Campaigns or events that are in draft or pending moderation.

---

## 💡 Practical Privacy Recommendations

1. **For Donors**: If you wish to support campaigns privately, check the **Donate anonymously** box during checkout or disable **Display Donation Badges** in privacy settings.
2. **For Event Organizers**: Keep **Show Organized Events** enabled so attendees can easily discover your complete event catalog.
3. **For Organizations**: Keep your profile set to **Public** and allow **Search Engine Indexing** to maximize donor reach and ticket sales.
