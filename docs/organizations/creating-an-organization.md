# Creating an Organization on Aldriva

Organizations on Aldriva represent public entities, nonprofits, businesses, churches, schools, creator brands, and community groups. Creating an organization provides you with a dedicated public hub, custom URL slug, and management dashboard for hosting events and launching fundraising campaigns.

---

## 🏢 When to Create an Organization

Create an organization if you:
- Operate a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, charity, or NGO.
- Host ticketed community events, conferences, or festivals.
- Run a local business, social enterprise, or corporate sponsorship program.
- Lead a church, religious institution, or community congregation.
- Represent an educational institution, school, or student association.
- Operate a creator collective, sports club, or civic group.

---

## 🚀 Step-by-Step Creation Guide

1. Log in to your Aldriva account and click **Create Organization** or visit `/create-organizer`.
2. **Organization Details**:
   - **Organization Name**: The official public name of your entity.
   - **URL Slug**: A unique URL path for your public hub (e.g., `aldriva.com/org/your-org-name`).
   - **Organization Type**: Choose the category that best matches your organization (see list below).
   - **Bio / Tagline**: A concise description of your mission, community focus, or services (up to 500 characters).
3. **Branding Imagery**:
   - **Logo / Photo**: Square logo (recommended: 400x400px, JPG/PNG).
   - **Cover Banner**: Wide header image (recommended: 1200x400px).
4. **Contact & Social Handles**:
   - Enter your public website URL and contact email.
   - Add your official social media handles (Facebook, X/Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok).
5. Click **Create Organization** to publish your public profile.

---

## 📋 Supported Organization Types

Aldriva supports the following entity classifications:

- **Nonprofit**: Registered charities, foundations, and 501(c)(3) organizations.
- **Business**: Companies, social enterprises, and commercial entities.
- **Church**: Religious congregations, ministries, and faith-based groups.
- **School**: Universities, primary/secondary schools, and educational programs.
- **Creator**: Independent artists, performers, content creators, and media brands.
- **Community**: Neighborhood associations, civic clubs, and grassroots groups.
- **Government**: Municipal departments, public agencies, and civic bodies.
- **Sports Club**: Athletic leagues, fitness teams, and community sports clubs.
- **Restaurant**: Food service providers, cafes, and culinary brands.
- **Other**: General groups and multi-purpose organizations.

> [!NOTE]
> All organization types share the standard core profile features (Overview, Events, Campaigns, and About). Specialized vertical tools (such as restaurant table reservations or medical booking) are not active.

---

## 🛡️ Next Steps: Verification & Capabilities

Once your organization is created:
1. **Public Hub**: Your public page is immediately live at `/org/[slug]`.
2. **Organization Dashboard**: Access your management hub at `/dashboard/org/[id]`.
3. **Verification**: To unlock official trust badges and organization fundraising, complete [Organization Verification](organization-verification.md).
