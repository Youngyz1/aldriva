# User Profiles on Aldriva

Every registered member on Aldriva receives a public member profile at `/profile/[id]`. Your profile represents your personal identity across the community, highlighting your public initiatives, organizations, and charitable contributions.

---

## 🌟 What Appears on Your Public Profile

When visitors or community members view your public profile at `/profile/[id]`, they see:

1. **Member Header**:
   - **Display Name**: Your chosen public name (or "Aldriva Member" if unset).
   - **Profile Avatar**: Your custom profile photo or a branded initial placeholder.
   - **Follower & Following Counts**: Real-time counts of members following you and organizations/users you follow.
   - **Follow Button**: Allows other logged-in members to follow your activity with a single click (hidden when viewing your own profile).

2. **Public Organizations**:
   - Up to 6 active organizations that you own and have marked public, complete with logo, name, verified badge (if verified), and bio.

3. **Public Campaigns**:
   - Fundraisers created by your account. Public visitors only see published campaigns with cover images, category badges, goal amounts, and raised amounts. When viewing your own profile, you also see your pending or in-review campaigns.

4. **Impact / Public Donation Activity**:
   - Highlights up to 6 recent public donations you have made to published campaigns.
   - Displays the contribution amount, fundraiser title, link to the campaign, and donation date.

---

## 🔒 What Remains Private

To protect your privacy and security, public member profiles **never** display:
- Your email address (unless you explicitly choose to display it in privacy settings).
- Your phone numbers, job titles, or company details.
- Your home, billing, shipping, or work addresses.
- Your identity verification documents or government ID details.
- Donations you made anonymously or donations to unpublished/private campaigns.
- Your payment methods, bank accounts, or payout configurations.

For fine-grained control over your visibility, see the [Profile Privacy & Settings Guide](profile-privacy-and-settings.md).

---

## ✏️ Editing Your Profile

You can customize your profile details at any time from your account settings:

1. Log in and navigate to **Settings → Profile** (`/dashboard/settings/profile`).
2. **Profile Photo**:
   - Click **Choose File** to select an image from your device.
   - Use the interactive round cropping tool to position your avatar.
   - Supported formats: JPG, PNG, WebP (up to 5MB).
3. **Display Name & Contact Information**:
   - Update your **Display Name** — this is the primary name shown across public comments, organizer cards, and your profile header.
   - Update your optional personal contact fields (Prefix, First Name, Last Name, Phone, Job Title, Company, Website, Blog Link).
4. **Address Records**:
   - Manage separate address records under the **Home**, **Billing**, **Shipping**, and **Work** tabs.
5. Click **Save Profile Details** to persist your changes.
