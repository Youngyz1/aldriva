# Organization Verification & Trust Badges

Organization Verification is the formal review process that grants public trust badges, enables organization fundraising, and activates payment rails on Aldriva.

---

## 🛡️ Why Verify Your Organization?

- **Verified Check Badge**: A public `🛡️ Verified` badge is displayed on your organization hub, event listings, and search results.
- **Fundraising Approval**: Unlocks the ability to launch and manage official organization fundraising campaigns.
- **Direct Payouts**: Enables direct banking and payment processing for your entity.
- **Donor & Attendee Trust**: Assures supporters that funds and ticket sales go directly to a legitimate organization.

---

## 📋 Document Requirements by Organization Type

Verification requirements are tailored to your specific organization classification:

### 1. Nonprofit / Charity
- **Registration Certificate or 501(c)(3) Letter (Required)**: Official government registration, charity commission certificate, or IRS determination letter.
- **Authorized Representative Photo ID (Required)**: Valid passport or driver's license of a legal officer, trustee, or director.
- **EIN / Tax Confirmation Document (Optional)**: Official tax authority letter confirming tax ID number.

### 2. Business / Company
- **Business License / Certificate of Incorporation (Required)**: Official company registration or trade license.
- **Director / Owner Photo ID (Required)**: Government ID of the legal owner or director.
- **Tax Registration (Optional)**: VAT / Sales tax registration certificate.

### 3. Church / Religious Institution
- **Church Registration / Letter of Standing (Required)**: Official denomination charter or religious organization certificate.
- **Authorized Representative Photo ID (Required)**: Government ID of senior pastor, trustee, or administrator.

### 4. School / Educational Institution
- **School Accreditation / Department License (Required)**: Department of Education license or accreditation certificate.
- **School Official Photo ID (Required)**: Government ID of principal, dean, or authorized officer.

### 5. Government Agency
- **Official Government Notice or Letterhead (Required)**: Letterhead authorization or official gazette notice confirming agency status.
- **Official Representative Photo ID (Required)**: Government credentials or passport of authorized officer.

### 6. Creator / Community / Sports Club / Other
- **Government-Issued Photo ID (Required)**: Valid passport, driver's license, or national identity card of primary lead.
- **Organization Proof / Overview Document (Optional)**: Group charter, league affiliation, or activity records.

---

## 🚀 How to Submit Organization Verification

1. Navigate to your **Organization Dashboard → Verification** (`/dashboard/org/[id]/verify`).
2. Review the list of required documents generated for your organization type.
3. Upload clear copies of all required documents (PDF, JPG, PNG, WebP up to 10MB each).
4. Enter any additional context in the notes field.
5. Click **Submit for Verification**.

---

## ⚖️ Understanding Verification Outcomes & Capabilities

Organization capabilities are evaluated independently:

1. **Organization Status (`status: 'verified'`)**:
   - Granted upon successful document review.
   - Activates the public **Verified Badge** across the platform.

2. **Fundraising Approval (`fundraising_approved: true`)**:
   - Authorizes the organization to create campaigns under the organization's byline.

3. **Payment Enablement (`payment_enabled: true`)**:
   - Activates direct withdrawal and disbursement capabilities.

---

## 🔒 Confidentiality of Organization Tax & Document Records

- **Tax ID & Registration Numbers**: Kept confidential and excluded from public hub queries.
- **Uploaded Documents**: Stored in secure, encrypted storage and reviewed only by compliance administrators.
