- **[Organization Dashboard](organization-dashboard.md)**: Manage connected events, campaigns, articles, products, reviews, settings, and understand upcoming modules.

- **[Personal Identity Verification](identity-verification.md)**: Submit government-issued identification to unlock secure payouts and protect your account.
- **[Organization Verification](organization-verification.md)**: Complete organization accreditation by entity type to earn verified badges and fundraising approval.
