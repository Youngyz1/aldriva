# Organization Management Dashboard

The **Organization Dashboard** (`/dashboard/org/[id]/*`) is the central operational command center for organization owners and authorized managers.

---

## 🎛️ Implemented Dashboard Sections

### 1. Overview (`/dashboard/org/[id]/overview`)
The main dashboard summary featuring:
- **KPI Stat Cards**:
  - **Total Events**: All upcoming and past events hosted.
  - **Fundraisers**: Active and completed crowdfunding campaigns.
  - **Followers**: Total community members following your organization.
  - **Total Raised**: Cumulative donations raised across all campaigns.
  - **Tickets Sold**: Total tickets issued across all events.
  - **Average Rating**: Community star rating and review count.
- **Recent Events List**: Quick table of recent events with links to edit details or view door operations.
- **Quick Action Bar**: One-click shortcuts to create an event, start a fundraiser, submit verification, or edit organization settings.

### 2. Events Management (`/dashboard/org/[id]/events`)
- View and manage all ticketed events attached to your organization.
- Launch the visual venue builder, guest roster, or door scanner apps.

### 3. Fundraisers Management (`/dashboard/org/[id]/fundraisers`)
- View all fundraising campaigns attached to your organization.
- Post backer updates, review donor rosters, and monitor progress toward financial goals.

### 4. Blog & Articles (`/dashboard/org/[id]/blog`)
- View articles and community impact stories published under your organization's byline.
- Direct link to compose new articles via the publishing suite.

### 5. Products (`/dashboard/org/[id]/products`)
- View digital goods, merchandise vouchers, and add-ons listed for sale by the organization.

### 6. Reviews (`/dashboard/org/[id]/reviews`)
- Dedicated feed of all verified community reviews and 1-to-5 star ratings submitted by attendees and donors.

### 7. Settings (`/dashboard/org/[id]/settings`)
- Update organization name, URL slug, biography, website, contact email, and social media handles.
- Securely update confidential registration numbers and Tax ID / EIN details.

### 8. Verification (`/dashboard/org/[id]/verify`)
- Access the multi-step [Organization Verification Wizard](organization-verification.md) to submit accreditation documents and track review status.

## 👥 Organization Team Roles & Access

Organization access is governed by role permissions:
- **Owner**: Full access to edit settings, submit verification, manage finances, and publish content.
- **Managers / Admins**: Authorized to manage events, fundraisers, and organization content.

> [!NOTE]
> While role-based permissions are enforced by the platform, self-service team member invitations are currently managed through platform administration.
