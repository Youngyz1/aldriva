# Following & Social Interactions

Aldriva features a lightweight, real-time social graph that allows community members to follow creators, organizers, and fellow supporters.

---

## 🤝 Following on Aldriva

You can follow two types of entities on Aldriva:

1. **Individual Members (`/profile/[id]`)**:
   - Follow community members to discover campaigns they create and support.
2. **Organizations (`/org/[slug]`)**:
   - Follow nonprofits, event organizers, churches, and creators to receive updates when they schedule new events or launch fundraisers.

---

## 🔘 How to Follow or Unfollow

1. Visit any public user profile (`/profile/[id]`) or organization hub (`/org/[slug]`).
2. Click the **Follow** button in the header.
3. The button immediately updates to **Following**, and the follower count increments in real time.
4. To unfollow, click the button again — it will instantly revert to **Follow** and adjust the count.

---

## 🔔 Real-Time In-App Notifications

When you follow an entity:
- **Following a User**: Sends an in-app notification to that member: *"New follower — [Your Name] started following you."* with a direct link to your profile.
- **Following an Organization**: Sends an in-app notification to the organization's owner alerting them to new community supporters.

---

## 📜 Social Graph Rules & Safeguards

- **Authentication Required**: You must be logged in to an active Aldriva account to follow users or organizations.
- **Self-Follow Prevention**: You cannot follow your own user profile or an organization that you own.
- **Duplicate Prevention**: The system automatically prevents duplicate follow relationships.
- **Privacy Assurance**: Following an entity does not grant that entity access to your private contact details, address, or payment records.
