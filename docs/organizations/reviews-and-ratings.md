# Reviews & Community Ratings

Community feedback and transparent ratings are essential for maintaining trust, safety, and accountability on Aldriva. Attendees and donors can leave verified reviews and 1-to-5 star ratings for events, campaigns, organizers, and the overall platform.

---

## ⭐ Supported Review Targets

You can submit feedback for:
1. **Platform Reviews (`/reviews`)**: Share your general experience using Aldriva for fundraising, ticketing, and community support.
2. **Organization Reviews**: Review an organizer's past events, communication, and community stewardship.
3. **Event Reviews**: Review specific events you attended.
4. **Fundraiser Reviews**: Leave feedback on campaign transparency and impact.

---

## ✍️ How to Submit a Review

1. Navigate to the public review section on the relevant page (or visit `/reviews` for general feedback).
2. **Star Rating**: Select an integer rating from **1 Star** (Poor) to **5 Stars** (Excellent).
3. **Review Title (Optional)**: A brief summary headline (up to 200 characters).
4. **Review Body (Optional)**: A detailed description of your experience (up to 2,000 characters).
5. Click **Submit Review**.

---

## 🛡️ Review Integrity & Anti-Spam Rules

- **Authentication Required**: You must be signed in to submit a review.
- **One Review Per Target**: Each member is permitted exactly one review per organization, event, fundraiser, or platform target. Attempting to submit a second review returns a duplicate notification.
- **Aggregate Rating Rollup**: Organization hubs display the true average rating and total approved review count (e.g., `★ 4.8 (24 reviews)`).
- **Moderation Standards**: All reviews are subject to platform community guidelines. Inappropriate language, spam, harassment, or fraudulent claims are subject to administrative removal.

---

## 📊 Viewing Reviews in the Organization Dashboard

Organization owners can monitor feedback in real time:
- Navigate to **Dashboard → Organization → Reviews** (`/dashboard/org/[id]/reviews`).
- Inspect all submitted reviews, star breakdowns, review text, and submission dates.
